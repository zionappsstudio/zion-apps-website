Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our sprints, code ownership, and delivery guarantees.

How can you deliver a working prototype in just 72 hours?
Traditional agencies spend weeks writing bloated documentation and holding endless discovery meetings. At JayTekton, we build on top of our production-tested, reusable architectural blueprints and leverage AI-assisted engineering velocity. Within 72 hours of receiving your core requirements, catalog, and brand assets, we assemble and deploy a clickable, interactive draft directly onto your phone.
Do I own the source code and data, or is there a monthly licensing fee?
You own 100% of the code, intellectual property, and database. Unlike proprietary SaaS platforms that charge per-seat or per-student subscriptions every month, we build your custom solution and hand over the entire GitHub repository and hosting credentials upon final project milestone clearance. You will never pay us a mandatory recurring licensing tax to run your own software.
What technology stack do you use to build our apps?
We engineer mobile applications using Flutter, Google's high-performance cross-platform engine that compiles to 60fps native code on both iOS and Android from a single codebase. For web platforms, we build modern, responsive interfaces backed by serverless cloud infrastructure (Firebase, Supabase, and Node.js microservices) ensuring bank-grade encryption, automated scaling, and zero server maintenance headaches.
What happens after the app goes live? Do you offer post-launch support?
Every production build includes 15 days of complimentary post-launch hypercare covering immediate bug fixes, server monitoring, and edge-case optimization. Following this window, you can retain us for ongoing monthly feature sprints or hand the clean, documented codebase over to your internal team with zero lock-in.
How does the payment milestone structure work?
We operate strictly on transparent milestone deliverables, never open-ended hourly billing:
• 50% Kickoff Advance: Secures your sprint slot and unlocks the 72-hour interactive prototype.
• 25% Staging Review: Due once backend logic, payment gateways, and core features are fully wired for your review.
• 25% Final Go-Live: Due upon final testing, domain DNS mapping, App Store / Play Store submission, and full repository handover.
Do I need my own Apple and Google Developer accounts to publish apps?
Yes, we guide you through setting up developer accounts in your organization's legal name so you retain full control over your app listings. Registration charges are applicable as per Google Play and Apple App Store policies. We handle the entire build packaging, screenshot assets, compliance forms, and submission process on your behalf.
Can you integrate our existing payment gateway or WhatsApp numbers?
Yes. We integrate with your direct merchant accounts—including Razorpay, Cashfree, Stripe, or direct UPI handles—so customer payments settle straight into your business bank account with 0% middleman deduction from us. We also configure official WhatsApp Cloud API webhooks using your verified business phone number.
